My 5 design tastes



1) Staggered entrances

Don't throw 8 elements at me at once.

Give them a rhythm.



2) Subtle hover states

A tiny movement can tell me:

“Yes, this is interactive.”



3) Shared transitions

When something moves between states, keep the visual connection.

It makes the interface feel intentional.



4) Scroll reveals

Not everything needs to arrive at the same time.

Guide the eye.



5) Micro-interactions

Small details.

Button press.

Card movement.

Text transition.

Nothing dramatic.

And that's the point.



Good animation should support the interface, not become the interface.
